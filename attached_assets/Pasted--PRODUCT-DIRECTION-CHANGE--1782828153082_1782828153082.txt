DealerPilot Marketplace AI should only generate optimized vehicle photography.

Delete:

- Ad Creative Generator
- Creative Templates
- Marketplace Standard
- Luxury Template
- Showroom Template

====================================================
NEW PRODUCT STRUCTURE
====================================================

Rename the module:

AI Vehicle Studio

This module has ONE purpose:

Transform dealership inventory photos into premium Marketplace-ready vehicle photos.

The workflow becomes:

1. Analyze all vehicle photos.
2. Choose the best source image automatically.
3. Remove dealer branding.
4. Remove promotional banners.
5. Remove phone numbers.
6. Remove financing graphics.
7. Reconstruct hidden vehicle parts.
8. Improve lighting.
9. Improve paint reflections.
10. Generate premium Marketplace-ready photography.

No text.

No CTA.

No prices.

No marketing graphics.

Only professional automotive photography.
