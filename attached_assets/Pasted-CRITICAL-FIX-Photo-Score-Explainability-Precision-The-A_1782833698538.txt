CRITICAL FIX — Photo Score Explainability + Precision

The AI photo scores look too generic and not trustworthy.

Many photos look similar but receive very different scores:
- some get 95 “Use Original”
- some get 50 “Do Not Use”
- some get 83 “Enhance Recommended”
without a clear visual explanation.

This makes the AI feel fake.

Goal:
Every photo score must be explainable, specific, and based on visible image quality signals.

====================================================
PHOTO SCORE MUST SHOW BREAKDOWN
====================================================

Each vehicle/photo card must show a score breakdown:

Overall Photo Score: 83/100

Breakdown:
- Vehicle visibility: 18/20
- Angle quality: 17/20
- Lighting: 13/15
- Sharpness: 12/15
- Background cleanliness: 8/15
- Branding / overlays: 5/10
- Crop / framing: 7/10
- Marketplace trust: 8/10

Total = 88/100

Do not show a score without the breakdown.

====================================================
SCORING RULES
====================================================

1. Vehicle Visibility
High score if full vehicle is visible.
Low score if:
- vehicle is cropped
- only partial front/back visible
- vehicle too far away
- vehicle blocked

2. Angle Quality
Best:
- front 3/4
- rear 3/4
- clean side angle
Worst:
- straight front too close
- roof-only
- interior-only as cover
- awkward crop

3. Lighting
Penalize:
- too dark
- blown out highlights
- strong reflections
- poor white balance
- heavy shadows

4. Sharpness
Penalize:
- blurry
- low resolution
- compression artifacts
- motion blur

5. Background Cleanliness
Penalize:
- busy dealer wall
- visible phone numbers
- promo banners
- clutter
- people
- other cars blocking view

6. Branding / Overlays
Penalize heavily if:
- Alpha banner is visible
- phone number is visible
- financing text is visible
- big dealer logo is visible
- promotional text blocks the vehicle

7. Crop / Framing
Penalize:
- vehicle cut off
- too much empty space
- bad perspective
- not centered

8. Marketplace Trust
High score if photo looks like a real clean vehicle photo.
Low score if it looks like a flyer, promo image, or over-branded dealer graphic.

====================================================
DECISION LOGIC
====================================================

Do not label “Use Original” just because score is high.

Decision must be:

90–100:
Use Original only if branding/overlay penalty is low.

75–89:
Enhance Recommended if branding is visible or background is messy.

60–74:
Enhance Recommended or Review Photo.

Under 60:
Do Not Use only if crop/visibility/sharpness is bad.

Important:
A photo with strong dealer branding should NOT be “Use Original” even if lighting and angle are good.
It should be:
Enhance Recommended
Reason:
Excellent angle, but dealer branding and financing banners reduce Marketplace trust.

On every photo/vehicle card show:

====================================================
AUDIT MODAL
====================================================

When clicking Open Audit:
Show the full explanation.

Include:
- selected cover photo
- score breakdown
- reason for decision
- what caused penalties
- whether enhancement is needed
- whether original should be preserved

====================================================
IMPORTANT
====================================================

If the scoring is currently simulated or heuristic, label it as:
“AI-estimated photo score”

Do not pretend it is true computer vision unless actual image analysis is being performed.

If possible, upgrade the logic to inspect image dimensions, brightness, contrast, blur, and OCR/text detection.

But even if still heuristic, the output must explain itself.

Acceptance:
Every score must have a visible reason.
Similar-looking photos should not receive wildly different labels unless the explanation justifies it.
Photos with large Alpha promotional banners should usually be “Enhance Recommended,” not “Use Original.”
