# DealerPilot AI — Sprint 4.6

## Mission Control UX Refinement

Sprint 4.5 is complete.

Do NOT build new features.

Do NOT create new routes.

Do NOT modify the backend.

Do NOT modify APIs.

Do NOT modify database schema.

Do NOT touch the Chrome Extension.

Do NOT touch workers.

This sprint is **100% UI/UX refinement**.

The objective is simple:

DealerPilot should feel like the AI operating system for dealerships.

Not a dashboard.

Not an admin panel.

An AI copilot.

---

# IMPORTANT

I have attached multiple screenshots of Autolander.

These screenshots are **UX inspiration only**.

Do NOT copy their layout.

Do NOT copy their branding.

Do NOT recreate their UI.

Study them only to understand:

• simplicity

• hierarchy

• spacing

• action-first workflow

DealerPilot must become its own product.

More premium.

More AI-first.

More enterprise.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Everything should answer one question:

"What should I do next?"

Every screen should naturally guide the operator toward the next action.

Reduce cognitive load.

Reduce clicks.

Increase whitespace.

Increase typography hierarchy.

Remove visual noise.

---

# 1 — SALES HUB

Completely rethink the Sales Hub.

It should become the AI Mission Control.

Not a statistics page.

Instead of showing raw metrics like:

256 Vehicles

53 Updates

18 Listings

Convert everything into AI recommendations.

Examples:

DealerPilot recommends publishing 18 vehicles today.

5 listings need renewal.

3 vehicles require price updates.

12 Marketplace conversations are waiting for an AI response.

Every recommendation must end with ONE primary CTA.

Publish Now

Update Prices

Renew Listings

Review Leads

Generate Creatives

Never place two equally-important buttons inside the same card.

One action.

One decision.

---

# 2 — VEHICLE CARDS

Status belongs on the image.

Every vehicle card should support overlay badges.

Examples:

LIVE

NEW

PRICE DROP

AI GENERATED

READY

QUEUED

PUBLISHED

RENEW

SOLD

NEEDS REVIEW

These badges should appear in the image corners.

The operator should understand vehicle status before reading text.

---

# 3 — MARKETPLACE AI

Inventory

Listings

Publishing

These are one workflow.

Keep existing backend architecture.

But redesign the UI into one workspace.

Tabs only.

Ready

Generating

Publishing

Published

Renewals

Needs Update

Sold

Switching tabs must feel instant.

No additional navigation.

---

# 4 — SIDEBAR

Simplify navigation.

Maximum six primary items.

Recommended:

Sales Hub

Marketplace AI

Creative AI

Sales AI

Dealer DNA

Settings

Move Connections, System Configuration, Control Center and admin utilities into Settings.

The sidebar should represent the operator's daily workflow.

Nothing else.

---

# 5 — GLOBAL HEADER

Persistent top bar.

Always visible.

Display:

Current Dealer

Facebook Connection

Chrome Extension

Last Inventory Sync

AI Status

Never force the user to open another screen just to verify system health.

# 7 — LIVE AI

Whenever AI is working...

The interface should feel alive.

No generic loading spinners.

Show progress.

Examples:

Generating Listings

Vehicle 8 of 25

Progress Bar

Estimated Remaining Time

Publishing to Marketplace

Waiting for Chrome Extension

Completed

The user should always know what DealerPilot is doing.

---

# 8 — AI VOICE

DealerPilot speaks.

Do not use spreadsheet language.

Avoid labels like:

256 Vehicles

12 Listings

3 Updates

Replace with AI language.

Examples:

DealerPilot found 18 vehicles ready to publish.

DealerPilot recommends updating prices on 3 vehicles.

DealerPilot generated 6 new creatives today.

12 buyers are waiting for a response.

The dashboard should feel like an intelligent assistant.

---

# 9 — TYPOGRAPHY

Use stronger visual hierarchy.

Small colored eyebrow labels.

Large bold titles.

One accent word.

Example:

MARKETPLACE

Publishing

Or

SALES

Mission Control

Increase whitespace.

Reduce borders.

Use shadows more than outlines.

No card should simply display information.

Every card should drive action.
