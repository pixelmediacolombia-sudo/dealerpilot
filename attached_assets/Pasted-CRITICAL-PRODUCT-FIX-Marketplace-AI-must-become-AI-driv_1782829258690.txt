CRITICAL PRODUCT FIX — Marketplace AI must become AI-driven

Marketplace AI still feels manual and basic.

Goal:
Marketplace AI should help me decide which vehicles to publish, why to publish them, what photo to use, and what strategy to use.

====================================================
PART 1 — Vehicle Photo Slideshow
====================================================

Every vehicle card in Marketplace AI must show a slideshow/gallery using ALL XML photos.

Requirements:
- If vehicle has 20 photos, the card should allow viewing all 20.
- Add left/right arrows on image hover.
- Add photo counter: 1 / 20.
- Add small dots or thumbnail strip if space allows.
- Show badge: Photos: 20.
- Preserve current primary image but allow browsing the full gallery.
- Do not only show the first photo.

====================================================
PART 2 — AI Cover Recommendation
====================================================

DealerPilot must automatically recommend the best photo for Marketplace cover.

Each vehicle card should show:
- AI Pick badge on recommended cover photo
- Photo Score
- Reason:
  - best angle
  - clean background
  - full vehicle visible
  - strong lighting
  - low branding
  - high buyer trust

If original photo is good:
“Use original. No enhancement needed.”

If bad:
“Enhance cover before publishing.”

====================================================
PART 3 — AI Publish Recommendation
====================================================

Do not recommend vehicles only by name.

DealerPilot must rank vehicles using strategy signals.

For each vehicle show:
- Publish Priority: High / Medium / Low
- Reason
- Strategy
- Recommended Down Payment
- Recommended Posting Window
- Recommended Creative Action

Ranking signals:
- price range
- body style
- mileage
- inventory age
- photo score
- listing score
- vehicle demand category
- affordability
- luxury/performance
- truck/SUV demand
- price changes
- sold/removed status
- existing published status

Example recommendations:

High Priority:
“Publish today. SUV under 40k with strong photos and high buyer demand.”

Medium Priority:
“Publish after cover photo enhancement. Good inventory, but current cover has heavy branding.”

Low Priority:
“Wait. Price is high for Marketplace and photo quality is weak.”

====================================================
PART 4 — Strategy Types
====================================================

Replace generic recommendations like:
- Full Price
- $500 down
- Monday 2pm

With real strategy labels:

- Fast Turn Strategy
- Serious Buyer Down Payment
- Premium SUV Strategy
- Truck Demand Strategy
- Luxury Trust Strategy
- Price Review Needed
- Photo Improvement First
- Use Original Photos
- Publish Today
- Hold for Review

====================================================
PART 5 — Down Payment Rules
====================================================

Never default to $500 down.

Use realistic rules:
- Under $20k: $1,500–$2,000
- $20k–$35k: $2,000–$3,000
- $35k–$50k: $3,000–$4,000
- $50k+: $4,000–$6,000
- Trucks/SUVs: add +$500
- Luxury/performance: add +$500–$1,000

Show reason:
“Higher-ticket SUV requires stronger buyer commitment.”

====================================================
PART 6 — AI Batch Suggestions
====================================================

At the top of Marketplace AI, add an AI recommendation block:

DealerPilot recommends publishing:
- 8 vehicles today
- 3 vehicles after photo enhancement
- 2 vehicles after price review

Add CTA:
Create AI Publishing Batch

Batch should automatically select recommended vehicles, not just whatever I click manually.

====================================================
PART 7 — UI
====================================================

Vehicle cards should show:

- Photo slideshow
- AI recommended cover
- Publish Priority
- Strategy label
- Recommended down
- Photo Score
- Listing Score
- Reason
- Primary CTA:
  Publish / Generate Listing / Enhance Photo / Review Price

Do not clutter with many buttons.
Use overflow menu for secondary manual actions.
