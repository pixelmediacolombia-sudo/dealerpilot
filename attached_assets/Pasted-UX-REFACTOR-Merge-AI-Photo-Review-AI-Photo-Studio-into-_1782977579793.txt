UX REFACTOR — Merge AI Photo Review + AI Photo Studio into one vehicle photo experience

Current problem:
The vehicle photo page has an “AI Photo Review” panel that says Coming Soon, while AI Photo Studio exists somewhere else.

This feels duplicated and confusing.

AI Vehicle / Vehicle Photos / AI Photo Studio should not feel like three separate modules doing the same thing.

Fix the UX:

====================================================
1. Replace AI Photo Review with AI Photo Studio
====================================================

On the vehicle photo detail page, replace the current “AI Photo Review” card with:

AI Photo Studio

This panel should show the live AI photo pipeline for the current vehicle.

States:

- Not processed
- Queued
- Processing
- AI Photos Ready
- Failed / Needs Review

====================================================
2. Auto-start AI enhancement from this panel
====================================================

If vehicle is Ready and has no AI Photo Set:

Show button:
“Generate AI Photos”

If background processing is enabled:

Automatically enqueue the vehicle and show:
“Generating AI photos…”

If AI photos are already ready:

Show:
“AI Photos Ready ✓”

====================================================
3. Show real AI output here
====================================================

The main photo preview should support two modes:

Original Photos
AI Enhanced Photos

Toggle:

[ Original ] [ AI Enhanced ]

When AI Enhanced is selected:
- show processed AI Photo Set
- exterior photos with Alpha Studio background
- interior photos enhanced only
- technical photos enhanced lightly / original if needed

====================================================
4. Before / After comparison
====================================================

Inside the AI Photo Studio panel show:

- Original thumbnail
- AI output thumbnail
- Classification
- Status
- Fallback indicator
- Processing time
- Confidence

For exterior:
Before / After comparison.

For interior:
Original / Enhanced comparison.

====================================================
5. Make AI Photo Studio vehicle-specific
====================================================

The AI Photo Studio page can still exist globally for queue/stats.

But the vehicle page must be the main place to review that vehicle’s AI photos.

Global AI Photo Studio = operations dashboard.
Vehicle AI Photo Studio = review and output.

====================================================
6. Remove duplicate Coming Soon language
====================================================

Delete:
“Coming Soon”
“Pending AI Review”
fake scoring placeholders

Replace with real status from ai_photo_sets and ai_photo_images.

====================================================
7. Marketplace publishing integration
====================================================

In this same panel show:

Marketplace will use:
- AI Enhanced Photos if Ready
- Original Photos if AI not ready

Add status badge:

Using AI Photos for Marketplace ✓
or
Using Original Photos — AI not ready

====================================================
8. Remove published vehicles from recommendations
====================================================

When a vehicle is Published:
- remove it from Today’s Recommendations
- leave the remaining 2 opportunities
- refresh list automatically

====================================================
Acceptance
====================================================

On the vehicle photo page:
- AI Photo Studio replaces AI Photo Review.
- Operator can see original vs AI photos.
- Operator can trigger AI photo generation.
- Operator can see processing status.
- Marketplace clearly shows whether it will use AI photos or originals.
- No duplicate or fake AI review placeholders.
