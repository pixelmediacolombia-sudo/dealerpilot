CRITICAL FIX — Photo AI scoring is over-penalizing dealer branding

DealerPilot is currently marking many photos as:
“Enhance Recommended”
Reason: “Dealer overlays visible”

This is wrong.

Dealer branding / Alpha wall / dealership background should NOT automatically trigger enhancement.

DealerPilot should recommend enhancement only when the photo quality hurts Marketplace performance.

New rule:
Dealer branding is acceptable if:
- it does not block the vehicle
- it does not cover the car body
- it does not reduce buyer trust
- it looks like a normal dealership photo
- the vehicle is clearly visible

Enhancement should be recommended only for real photo quality issues:
- vehicle cropped
- vehicle too dark
- blurry photo
- bad angle
- poor framing
- heavy reflections
- low sharpness
- vehicle partially blocked
- bad exposure
- messy background that distracts from the car
- promotional overlay covering the vehicle
- text/phone/banner physically covering important parts of the vehicle

Important:
Dealer wall branding in the background is NOT the same as an overlay covering the vehicle.

Examples:
1. Alpha logo on wall behind car:
Do NOT automatically enhance.
Score can still be high.

2. Yellow banner covering bottom of image but not blocking vehicle:
Minor penalty only.
Do NOT automatically enhance unless other issues exist.

3. Text/phone/logo covering the car body:
Enhance recommended.

4. Photo is dark/blurry/cropped:
Enhance recommended.

Update photo decision logic:
- Use Original if vehicle visibility, angle, lighting and sharpness are strong, even with dealer background.
- Enhance Recommended only if photo quality issues meaningfully reduce Marketplace trust.
- Do Not Use only for truly bad photos.

Also stop giving every vehicle the same score like 77.
Scores must vary based on actual photo factors, not a repeated fallback.

Each card should show:
- Photo Score
- Decision
- Main reason
- Top positive signal
- Top negative signal

Acceptance:
A clean Alpha showroom photo with the car clearly visible should often be Use Original.
Dealer overlays visible alone should not be enough for Enhance Recommended.
