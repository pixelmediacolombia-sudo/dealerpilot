SPRINT 7.5 — AI Vehicle Studio Redesign (Marketplace First)

DealerPilot is NOT an image generator.

DealerPilot is an AI Marketplace publishing assistant.

The current AI Vehicle Studio is wrong because it generates multiple fake versions (Luxury, Studio, White Background, Transparent PNG, etc.).

Marketplace users do not need multiple creative versions.

They need ONE optimized Marketplace-ready photo.

Rebuild the AI Vehicle Studio around that philosophy.

OBJECTIVE

Redesign the entire Photo AI workflow.

The AI must analyze all source photos, choose the best cover automatically, decide if enhancement is actually needed, and generate only ONE enhanced version when required.

The UI must look like an enterprise AI auditing platform, not an image generator.
