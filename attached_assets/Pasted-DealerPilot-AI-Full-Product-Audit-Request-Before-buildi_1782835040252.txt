DealerPilot AI — Full Product Audit Request

Before building more features, I need a complete audit report of the current app.

Do NOT implement changes.
Do NOT refactor.
Do NOT redesign.
Do NOT modify backend, database, APIs, UI, extension, or workers.

Your job is to inspect the current product and deliver a detailed audit report.

Context:
DealerPilot AI should become a semi-automatic AI operator for car dealers.
The app should use AI strategically to:
- decide which vehicles to publish
- choose the best photos
- recommend pricing/down payment strategy
- create publishing batches
- reduce manual decisions
- assist Marketplace publishing
- learn from engagement and leads

I need the audit to evaluate whether the current app actually supports that vision.
