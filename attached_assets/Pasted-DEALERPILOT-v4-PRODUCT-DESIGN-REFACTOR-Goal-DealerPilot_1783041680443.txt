DEALERPILOT v4 — PRODUCT DESIGN REFACTOR

Goal:
DealerPilot must stop looking like a generic AI dashboard.
It should feel like a premium AI operations cockpit for auto dealerships.

Do not add new features.
Redesign the information architecture and UI around a unique product identity.

====================================================
1. Reduce sidebar to 6 core modules
====================================================

Keep only:

1. Command Center
2. Marketplace
3. Inventory
4. AI Photo Studio
5. Sales AI
6. Dealer DNA

Move Settings to the bottom avatar/user menu.

Remove or merge:
- Intelligence → merge into Marketplace
- AI Vehicle Studio → merge into AI Photo Studio
- AI Photo Review → replace with AI Photo Studio inside vehicle detail

====================================================
2. Command Center becomes the daily mission screen
====================================================

Replace the current dashboard with:

Header:
“Good evening, Operator.”

Main mission cards:
- Vehicles ready
- Listings live
- Buyers waiting
- Appointments today
- Issues needing attention

Primary action:
“Publish Next Best Vehicle”

Daily mission section:
- Publish 3 vehicles
- Reply to new buyers
- Review failed jobs
- Check AI photos

No clutter.
No duplicate widgets.
No technical queue unless expanded.

====================================================
3. Marketplace becomes the publishing cockpit
====================================================

Merge Marketplace AI + Intelligence into one module:

Marketplace

Sections:
- Today’s Picks
- Publishing Queue
- Published
- Needs Review
- Marketplace Health

Cards should be premium, wide, low clutter.

Each vehicle card:
- photo
- vehicle
- price
- AI score
- strategy badge
- publish button
- preview button

No repeated dashboards.

====================================================
4. Inventory becomes visual and scannable
====================================================

Inventory cards/table should emphasize:

- vehicle photo
- price
- mileage
- photos count
- AI photo status
- marketplace status
- lead activity

Use badges instead of long text.

====================================================
5. AI Photo Studio becomes the media engine
====================================================

AI Photo Studio is the only place for:
- classification
- background replacement
- enhancement
- before/after
- quality review
- photo set versions

Inside Vehicle Detail, replace AI Photo Review with embedded AI Photo Studio.

Tabs:
- Original
- AI Enhanced
- Before / After
- Processing
- Quality

Show:
- AI photo status
- active photo set
- marketplace uses AI photos yes/no
- cost
- processing time

No “Coming Soon” placeholders.

====================================================
6. Sales AI becomes conversation-first
====================================================

Do not use ugly CRM cards.

Make it feel like an inbox.

Layout:
Left column:
- buyer conversations

Center:
- conversation thread

Right:
- vehicle, lead score, appointment, quick actions

Quick actions:
- Reply
- Call
- Appointment
- Sold
- Send listing

====================================================
7. Visual identity
====================================================

Keep dark mode, but make it premium.

Inspiration:
- Linear
- Arc Browser
- Apple Vision Pro
- Stripe Dashboard
- Tesla service software

Style:
- more spacing
- fewer borders
- larger typography
- soft gradients
- module-specific accent colors
- smoother cards
- less red unless critical
- less clutter

Module accents:
- Command Center: electric blue
- Marketplace: green
- Inventory: cyan
- AI Photo Studio: gold
- Sales AI: violet
- Dealer DNA: orange

====================================================
8. Sidebar redesign
====================================================

Sidebar should feel premium and minimal.

Use:
- clean icons
- module names
- active glow
- no heavy boxes
- bottom operator profile

The app should not feel like a generic shadcn admin panel.

====================================================
9. Motion and feedback
====================================================

Add subtle motion:
- processing steps animate
- published success state
- AI photo ready state
- queue progress

No distracting animation.
Premium, soft, fast.

====================================================
10. Remove duplicated states
====================================================

Do not show the same data in 3 places.

One source of truth:
- Marketplace owns publishing
- AI Photo Studio owns photos
- Sales AI owns messages
- Inventory owns vehicles
- Command Center summarizes everything

====================================================
11. Acceptance
====================================================

After refactor:

- Sidebar has only 6 modules
- Command Center feels like a daily mission screen
- Marketplace and Intelligence are merged
- AI Vehicle Studio and AI Photo Studio are merged
- Vehicle detail embeds AI Photo Studio
- Sales AI feels like an inbox, not broken cards
- App looks premium and custom, not generic AI dashboard
- No fake Coming Soon panels
- No duplicated modules
