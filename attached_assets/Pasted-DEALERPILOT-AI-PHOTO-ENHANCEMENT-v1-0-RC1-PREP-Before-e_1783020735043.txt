====================================================
PHASE 1 — AI PHOTO ENHANCEMENT v2
====================================================

Current problem:
The enhancement looks over-processed.
It relies too much on sharpening / CLAHE / normalize.
The images look crunchy, artificial, and filtered.

The goal is NOT sharper images.

The goal is professional dealership photography.

The enhanced version should look like the dealer hired a professional photographer.

Keep original background.
Do NOT replace background.
Do NOT remove background.
Do NOT change the vehicle.

====================================================
ENHANCEMENT PRIORITY ORDER
====================================================

1. Noise reduction
2. JPEG artifact reduction
3. White balance correction
4. Exposure correction
5. Shadow recovery
6. Highlight recovery
7. Dynamic range improvement
8. Paint depth enhancement
9. Glass clarity enhancement
10. Chrome clarity
11. Tire/wheel contrast
12. Very subtle local sharpening
13. Final luxury dealership color grade

EXTERIOR PHOTO RULES
====================================================

Exterior photos should look:

- cleaner

SHARPNESS RULE
====================================================

Sharpening must be subtle and local.

Do NOT use heavy global sharpen.

Avoid:
- crunchy edges
- JPEG noise sharpened
- white halos
- artificial texture
- over-processed look

If an enhanced photo looks worse than the original, keep the original.
