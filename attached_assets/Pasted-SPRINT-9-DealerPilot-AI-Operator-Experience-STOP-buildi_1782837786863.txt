SPRINT 9 — DealerPilot AI Operator Experience

STOP building more dashboard features.

DealerPilot currently looks premium, but it still feels like a manual dashboard.

The product vision is different:

DealerPilot should feel like an AI Marketplace Operator that works for the dealer every day.

The user should not have to think:
“What should I publish?”
“What photo should I use?”
“What down payment should I post?”
“Which vehicles should I skip?”
“What should I do next?”

DealerPilot must answer those questions automatically.

====================================================
MAIN GOAL
====================================================

Transform the app from a dashboard into an AI operator.

Every major screen should answer:

“What should I do right now?”

The AI should:
- recommend vehicles to publish today
- explain why
- choose which vehicles NOT to publish
- avoid publishing duplicate similar vehicles together
- recommend Marketplace price/down payment
- recommend photo action
- create publishing jobs
- reduce buttons
- show one clear next action

====================================================
PART 1 — SALES HUB → COMMAND CENTER
====================================================

Sales Hub is currently too generic.

Rename or reposition Sales Hub as:

Command Center

The first screen must show:

DealerPilot Morning Brief

Example:

Good afternoon, Andrés.

DealerPilot found 3 opportunities today.

1. Publish 2022 Toyota Tacoma
Reason:
- Truck demand is strong
- High buyer intent segment
- 20 photos available
- Good Marketplace price
- Recommended down payment: $4,000
- Best posting window: 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Primary button:
Post to Marketplace

2. Publish 2020 Honda Accord
Reason:
- Affordable sedan
- Strong entry-level buyer demand
- Good price point
- Marketplace price should be down payment, not full price

Primary button:
Post to Marketplace

3. Hold 2025 Ford Mustang
Reason:
- High ticket performance car
- Better for weekend posting
- Needs stronger cover photo

Primary button:
Review Strategy

Do not show only:
Publish 0 Vehicles
Reply 0 Buyers
Update Inventory

Those are status cards, not AI guidance.

Command Center should include:
- Today’s top 3 publish recommendations
- Vehicles to avoid today
- Listings needing update
- Buyers needing reply
- Inventory changes
- One primary action per card

Data label:
Estimated Strategy until real Marketplace engagement exists.

====================================================
PART 2 — MARKETPLACE AI → MARKETPLACE OPERATOR
====================================================

Marketplace AI should not feel like a vehicle list.

It should feel like the AI is operating Marketplace.

At the top, add:

DealerPilot recommends publishing X vehicles today.

Show:
- top 3 vehicles
- why each one was chosen
- Marketplace price to post
- recommended down payment
- best photo status
- strategy label
- confidence/estimated priority
- button: Publish Now
- button: Add to Batch

Then show:
- Ready to publish
- Needs review
- Not recommended today
- Already published

For duplicate vehicles:
Example:
Honda Accord — 3 ready

DealerPilot should explain:
- publish only 1 today
- hold the other 2
- avoid competing against yourself
- rotate the others tomorrow or later

Requirements:
- If multiple same make/model are ready, group them.
- Show “Duplicate group detected.”
- AI should recommend which one to publish first.
- AI should explain why that unit wins:
  price, mileage, year, photo count, listing readiness, affordability.

====================================================
PART 3 — AI PUBLISHING DECISION ENGINE
====================================================

Create a centralized decision function/service.

Name it something like:

buildDailyMarketplacePlan()

Inputs:
- inventory vehicles
- vehicle price
- body style
- year
- mileage
- stock/VIN
- photo count
- listing status
- publishing status
- sold/removed status
- price changes
- Strategy Engine v2 recommendation
- down payment rule
- duplicate model groups
- pending jobs
- published listings
- available real engagement if any

Output:
{
  recommendedToday: [],
  holdToday: [],
  needsReview: [],
  duplicateGroups: [],
  publishOrder: [],
  summary: "",
  estimatedImpact: "",
  dataSource: "Estimated Strategy"
}

Do NOT use random numbers.
Do NOT use Math.sin.
Do NOT use list position.
Do NOT use seeded performance as real.

====================================================
PART 4 — MARKETPLACE PRICE RULE
====================================================

Apply this globally.

If vehicle actual price < $16,000:
- Marketplace displayed price = actual price
- priceMode = FULL_PRICE

If vehicle actual price >= $16,000:
- Marketplace displayed price = recommended down payment
- priceMode = DOWN_PAYMENT

The UI must show both:
Actual vehicle price
Marketplace price to post

Example:
Actual Price: $28,995
Marketplace Price: $3,000 down
Reason: Higher-ticket truck should be framed as down payment.

Chrome extension payload must use:
marketplaceDisplayedPrice

NOT actualVehiclePrice unless priceMode = FULL_PRICE.

====================================================
PART 5 — AI VEHICLE STUDIO → VISION AI
====================================================

AI Vehicle Studio should not be a placeholder page.

Until real computer vision exists, it must be honest.

Do not show fake photo scores.

Do not show fake confidence.

Do not say AI reviewed the photo if it did not.

Current state should say:
AI Photo Review Pending
Real computer vision required.

However, the page should still be useful.

Show:
- all photos
- photo count
- manual cover selection
- current cover
- selected cover
- image gallery
- interior/exterior filter if available
- “Use as Marketplace Cover”
- “Send to Publishing Batch”

Important:
Do NOT generate 7 outputs.
Do NOT show fake enhanced photos.
Do NOT say overlays were removed unless real image processing happened.

Future-ready copy:
“DealerPilot will analyze angle, lighting, sharpness, crop, and background once Vision AI is connected.”

====================================================
PART 6 — PHOTO STRATEGY WITHOUT FAKE AI
====================================================

Until real Vision AI is connected:

Use objective photo rules only:
- Photo count available
- Cover selected or not selected
- Original image exists
- Gallery exists
- Operator-selected cover

Do not score image quality.

Use labels:
- Cover selected
- Needs cover selection
- Gallery ready
- Pending Vision AI

Do not use:
- AI Score
- Photo Score
- Use Original
- Enhance Recommended
- Do Not Use

Unless real computer vision/image analysis is integrated.

====================================================
PART 7 — MARKETPLACE INTELLIGENCE SIMPLIFICATION
====================================================

Marketplace Intelligence has too many tabs.

Simplify into:

1. Recommendations
2. Performance
3. Opportunities

Recommendations:
- Estimated strategies based on real inventory rules
- Publish Now
- Add to Batch
- Schedule

Performance:
- Only real published listing data
- If none, show “No real Marketplace performance yet.”

Opportunities:
- price changes
- stale listings
- vehicles ready but not published
- duplicate model groups
- sold vehicles still published
- listings needing update

Keep clear banner:
“Demo intelligence — not real Marketplace performance yet”
when seeded analytics are visible.
