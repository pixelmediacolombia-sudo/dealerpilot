Add a Buyer Segment Fit Score.

This should influence Opportunity Score but not override price, mileage, availability, or real demand.

Suggested weight:
Buyer Segment Fit: 10%

To keep total score at 100, adjust weights:

Market Demand: 25%
Price: 18%
Vehicle Quality / Mileage: 12%
Buyer Segment Fit: 10%
Dealer Performance: 10%
Buyer Demand: 10%
Seasonality: 7%
Inventory Age: 5%
Creative / Photo Quality: 3%

Every recommendation should show:

- Opportunity Score
- Recommended buyer segment
- Why this audience may respond
- Suggested ad angle
- Suggested language:
  Spanish-first / English-first / bilingual
