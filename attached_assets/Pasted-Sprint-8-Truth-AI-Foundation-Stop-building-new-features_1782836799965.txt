Sprint 8 — Truth + AI Foundation

Stop building new features.

DealerPilot looks premium, but too many AI parts are fake, seeded, or heuristic. The next sprint must make the product honest, usable, and ready for real AI.

Goals:
1. Remove fake AI scores.
2. Remove fake Marketplace KPIs.
3. Remove fake photo enhancement claims.
4. Make Sales Hub answer: “What should I do today?”
5. Keep only real, estimated, or pending states clearly labeled.

Tasks:

1. Photo AI cleanup
- Remove all Math.sin / random / position-based photo scores.
- Do not show numeric AI scores until real vision is integrated.
- Replace with:
  Pending AI Review
  Use Original
  Needs Human Review
  Enhancement Available
- Do not claim enhancement unless a real image is generated.
- Generate only one enhanced photo when real enhancement exists.
- No 7 outputs.

2. Marketplace Intelligence cleanup
- Remove or hide fake KPIs:
  views
  conversations
  hot leads
  CTR
  outcome score
  posting performance
- If data is seeded, show a clear banner:
  “Demo intelligence — not real Marketplace performance yet.”
- Real metrics only:
  inventory count
  photo count
  listings generated
  publishing jobs
  published listings
  feed sync status

3. Sales Hub AI Daily Plan
Add a top section:
“DealerPilot recommends these actions today.”

Show:
- 3 vehicles to publish today
- why each was chosen
- marketplace price to post
- recommended down payment
- one button: Post to Marketplace

Use only real vehicle data + Strategy Engine rules.
Clearly label as “Estimated strategy” until real engagement exists.

4. Publishing workflow
- Make Publish button from Sales Hub and Marketplace Intelligence create a real publishing job.
- No undefined job IDs.
- Ready → Queue → Extension → Mark Published → Published tab.
- Published vehicles disappear from Ready.

5. Data labels
Every metric and recommendation must show source:
- Real Data
- Estimated
- Demo
- Pending AI

6. UI cleanup
- Fewer buttons.
- One primary action per card.
- Manual actions go to overflow.
- Sidebar stays fixed.
- Compact list view should be default for 100+ vehicles.

Acceptance:
- No fake scores.
- No fake KPIs shown as real.
- No 7 photo outputs.
- Sales Hub shows 3 publish-today recommendations.
- Publish buttons create real jobs.
- All data is labeled honestly.
- Typecheck clean.
