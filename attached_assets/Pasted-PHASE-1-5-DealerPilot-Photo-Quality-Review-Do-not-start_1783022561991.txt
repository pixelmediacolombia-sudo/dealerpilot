PHASE 1.5 — DealerPilot Photo Quality Review

Do not start RC1 yet.

The enhancement pipeline improved technically, but the validation is still engineering-focused.

Do not validate photo quality by sharpness percentage.

Sharpness ratio does not prove the image looks professional.

We need a photography-oriented quality review.

====================================================
GOAL
====================================================

DealerPilot must prove that the enhanced photo looks more valuable, more professional, and more marketplace-ready than the original.

Replace:

Sharpness Ratio

with:

DealerPilot Photo Quality Report

====================================================
QUALITY SCORE CATEGORIES
====================================================

For every enhanced image, evaluate Original vs Enhanced across:

1. Lighting
2. Exposure
3. White Balance
4. Dynamic Range
5. Paint Quality
6. Glass Quality
7. Interior Material Quality
8. Naturalness
9. Artifact Detection
10. Marketplace Readiness

Each category should score 0–100.

Also generate:

Overall Photo Quality Score
Marketplace Ready Score
Improvement Delta

Example:

Original:
Lighting 72
Exposure 75
Paint Quality 68
Naturalness 88
Marketplace Ready 74

Enhanced:
Lighting 91
Exposure 92
Paint Quality 89
Naturalness 94
Marketplace Ready 93

Improvement:
+19 points

====================================================
AI ANALYSIS TEXT
====================================================

For each image generate a short AI explanation.

Example:

Original:
- Slight underexposure
- Cool white balance
- Weak paint reflections
- Windshield haze
- JPEG compression visible

Enhanced:
✓ Corrected white balance
✓ Improved exposure
✓ Increased paint depth
✓ Reduced compression artifacts
✓ Improved glass clarity

====================================================
IMPORTANT
====================================================

Do NOT show technical terms like:
- Sharpness ratio
- CLAHE
- gamma
- m2
- provider
- Sharp.js

The dealer should see business/photo language, not engineering language.

====================================================
QUALITY GATE
====================================================

Enhanced image passes only if:

- Marketplace Ready Score >= 85
- Naturalness >= 85
- Artifact Detection >= 85
- Improvement Delta >= +5

If enhanced image fails:

- keep original
- mark image Needs Review
- show reason

Do not let bad enhanced photos become default.

Remove Sharpness Ratio from UI.

Photo Quality Report
