# UX Rules

Reduce buttons dramatically.

One primary action per screen.

Hide secondary actions inside:

• Context menus

• Hover menus

• Dropdowns

• Slide panels

• Right-click menus

• Expandable cards

Avoid visual overload.

---

Increase whitespace significantly.

Cards should breathe.

Sections should breathe.

Nothing should feel crowded.

---

Replace tables with beautiful cards whenever possible.

Image-first layouts.

Status badges.

Large typography.

Minimal text.

---

The dashboard should communicate decisions, not data.

Instead of showing:

53 Vehicles

Show:

AI recommends publishing 53 vehicles.

Instead of:

12 Messages

Show:

12 Marketplace buyers are waiting for a reply.

DealerPilot should feel like an AI employee, not a database.
