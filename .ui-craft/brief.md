## 1. Product purpose

DealerPilot coordinates vehicle inventory, Marketplace publishing, buyer conversations, photo production, and dealership operations from one workspace.

## 2. Primary user

An Alpha MotorSports operator working repeatedly throughout the day on a laptop, deciding what needs attention and acting on vehicles, listings, and buyers within seconds.

## 3. Principles

1. **The next action beats the impressive dashboard.** Operational queues and clear status always outrank decorative metrics.
2. **Show the work, not the AI.** DealerPilot should feel like dependable dealership software, never a futuristic cockpit or an AI landing page.
3. **One system, many workflows.** Inventory, Marketplace, studio, sales, and settings share the same neutral shell and soft-blue wayfinding accent.
4. **Status color must mean something.** Green, amber, and red are reserved for operational state; decoration stays neutral.
5. **Dense does not mean cramped.** Operators should scan quickly without tiny type, low-contrast labels, or hidden primary actions.

## 4. Success metric for the surface

An operator can identify the most urgent item and reach the correct workflow within 30 seconds of opening DealerPilot. The interface remains legible and fully navigable from mobile through desktop.

## 5. Out of scope

- Does not change backend contracts, business rules, or automation behavior.
- Does not rename established routes or operational concepts.
- Does not add decorative charts without a real data decision.
- Does not introduce a second component library.
- Does not turn product screens into marketing pages.

## 6. Learned constraints

- **2026-08-09** — Avoid the generic AI look: forced dark mode, blue-violet gradients, glows, glass panels, excessive cards, and repeated uppercase labels. *Why:* the user wants DealerPilot to read as professional operational software.
- **2026-08-09** — Use the restrained light dashboard direction demonstrated by UI Craft. *Why:* the user explicitly selected that before/after example.
- **2026-08-09** — The browser identity uses a DP monogram in the product accent. *Why:* the previous colored dot did not identify DealerPilot.
